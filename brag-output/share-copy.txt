A small accounting detail that quietly breaks project finance in India:

TDS is deducted by the client *before* they pay you. Most receivables
reports still leave it sitting in the outstanding column — so the number you
chase every month is larger than the number anyone actually owes you.

In our sample ledger that's ₹5,47,850 of ₹32,99,500. Sixteen percent of a
collections list, chasing money that was never coming.

FinTrack was built around that. Project delivery and the money for it on one
set of records — a live status board sitting on the same rows as the
invoices, GST and TDS reported separately, and an AI analyst that prints the
compiled SQL next to every answer, because on money an answer you can't check
isn't a faster answer.

signup and no demo call: [link]
